# Introduction

---

Welcome to the Assemble design system documentation. This guide will help you get up and running with our design tokens, components, and best practices.

![Releases cover](/covers/introduction.png)


## Powered by Material 3 Design System

Assemble is built on Google's Material 3 Design System and implemented using Flutter as our primary framework. By leveraging Material's powerful native mobile support and Flutter's cross‑platform scalability, we ensure our products maintain a unified look and feel while accelerating development.

Material gives us a solid, battle‑tested foundation—so the heavy lifting is already done, and we can focus on crafting high‑quality McAfee experiences.

## AI Driven Design System

Assemble is built to fit naturally into modern workflows—whether you're designing in Figma or developing in code. Our system stays in sync across design files and codebases, helping teams move quickly while maintaining alignment.
